Privacy Policy

App: Pitchside (package com.calebtek.pitchside) · Developer: Olawale Odeyemi Ebenezer (CalebTek) · Effective: 13 November 2026

In short: Pitchside does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

1. What the app stores

To run your tournaments, Pitchside saves the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Tournament names, formats and settings
  • Team or club names, and the player names you type in
  • Fixtures and the match scores you enter

This information is never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

2. What the app does not do

ItemPitchside
Accounts or sign-upNone
Internet connectionNot used. The app makes no network requests
Analytics, tracking or advertising IDsNone
AdsNone
Location, contacts, camera, microphone, photosNot accessed
Crash reporting or third-party SDKs that collect dataNone
Selling or sharing dataNever

3. Deleting your data

You are always in control. Delete a single tournament from its ⋯ menu inside the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Pitchside. Because no copy exists anywhere else, the deletion is complete and permanent.

4. Children

Pitchside is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

5. Third-party services

The app itself uses no third-party services. If you download Pitchside from an app store or from GitHub, that store or site handles the download under its own privacy policy. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

6. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that handle data differently (for example optional cloud sync, accounts or purchases), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/CalebTek/Pitchside/issues or contact the developer through github.com/CalebTek.
